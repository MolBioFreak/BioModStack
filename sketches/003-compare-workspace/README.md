## Variant: Compare workspace

### Design stance
Make source-versus-candidate interpretation the primary activity.

### Key choices
- Layout: paired source and candidate viewports with a candidate strip below.
- Typography: spacious headings with compact evidence cards.
- Color: cyan source, lime candidate, gold selected region.
- Interaction: side-by-side mode, overlay mode, candidate pinning, acceptance ledger, artifact actions.

### Trade-offs
- Strong at: understanding geometric change and preserving source context.
- Weak at: less efficient for broad request/provenance inspection.

### Best for
- Reviewing a candidate before downstream validation or experimental handoff.
